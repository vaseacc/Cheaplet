    Trust & Safety

    Cheaplet is built for students. Follow these guidelines to ensure your buying and selling experience is safe and successful.

    Meet in Public Places

    Always arrange to meet in busy, well-lit public areas. Campus cafeterias, student union buildings, and school libraries are excellent spots.

    Inspect Before You Pay

    Take the time to thoroughly inspect the textbook or item before handing over money. Check for missing pages, water damage, or excessive writing.

    Beware of Advance Payments

    We highly recommend paying in cash or completing an e-transfer only after you have met the seller in person and inspected the item. Avoid sending money to strangers online in advance.

    Report Suspicious Behavior

    If a user acts aggressively, attempts a scam, or makes you feel unsafe, click the red flag icon on their profile or chat. Our admin team reviews all reports immediately.
